# Community code of conduct

We want a practical, respectful community where domain practitioners and software contributors can work together.

Be constructive and specific. Welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Critique ideas and evidence without attacking people. Respect privacy, intellectual property and the limits of synthetic examples.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Never post confidential employer, supplier or customer information.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, request changes, restrict participation or ban repeated or severe violations. They should explain decisions proportionately, protect affected people's privacy and apply the rules consistently.

Report concerns through a private maintainer channel listed on the published repository. If none is configured, request a private contact channel without naming affected people publicly. The release checklist requires contact/reporting settings to be configured before launch.
